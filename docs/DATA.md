## grf_tracking_data

**Attribution.** "Simulating Tracking Data to Advance Sports Analytics Research, Radke & Tilbury, AAMAS 2025."
Repository: github.com/Dtradke/grf_tracking_data (commit `fe823ab`, 24 Mar 2025). Paper: arXiv 2503.19809.

**Licence.** The repository has no LICENSE file (checked 21 Oct 2026); the arXiv listing shows a CC0 badge, which
covers the paper. The data and any weights derived from it are kept out of this repository
(`packages/policy/artifacts/*.pt` is git-ignored).

**What was downloaded.** `tiny_set`: 4 CSV files, 12 games, 105 MB, fetched by the repository's own
`scripts/download_game_data.py` from the authors' Google Drive into `data/grf/tiny_set/`.

**What the check found** (`python3 packages/policy/grf_check.py data/grf/tiny_set`, full numbers in
`data/grf/coords_report.json`):

- Columns exactly as documented; 23 rows per timestep (22 players, ball `PLAYER_ID = -1`); TIMESTEP spacing 1;
  3,001 timesteps per game.
- Frame rate derived from the data: the ball's vertical acceleration in flight equals g at a step of 0.098 s,
  i.e. **10.2 Hz** (7,258 airborne samples), consistent with the expected 10 Hz. A 3,001-step game therefore covers
  about 300 s of play, not 90 minutes: GRF compresses a match. Per-minute rates (shots, passes) must be normalised
  per step, never compared to real per-90 numbers directly.
- The metre mapping holds: 99.997% of player positions lie within 0.02 of x in [-1, 1], y in [-0.42, 0.42]
  (observed players x [-1.046, 1.046], y [-0.446, 0.445]; ball z up to 15.5 m).
- Caveats: one normalised unit is 52.5 m along x but 80.95 m along y (anisotropic), so velocities are
  converted per axis; `VELOCITY_X/Y` are displacement per step, not per second; player `LOCATION_Z` and
  `VELOCITY_Z` are `-1` placeholders. Player p99 forward speed at 10.2 Hz is 7.7 m/s, a plausible sprint.

## Broadcast footage

Licensed Premier League highlight clips under `data/footage/` are covered by the written permission described in
`docs/permissions/README.md`. They are media, not datasets: never committed, never used
