## Provenance and review notes (written by track A, 22 Oct 2026)

- **Checkpoint:** epoch 2 of the first `policy_v1` run (best by re-validated val loss 2.855; kind accuracy 0.809),
  trained on the dataset built before the 22 Oct movement-realism fix to the engine. Offline metrics use that
  dataset's test split; the 20 rollouts use the current engine (regenerated evaluation seeds 1-20). A run on the
  regenerated dataset is training overnight; `python -m policy.evaluate` will refresh these numbers, and the
  decision cannot change unless top-3 more than recovers the gap below.
- **Why top-3 misses:** the scripted rule chooses among good passes almost evenly (temperature 0.012 over small
  utility gaps). Scoring the rule's own option probabilities against what it actually played gives only about
  **0.44** top-3 (0.43 on passes; its single most likely target averages 17%). The policy (0.505) already beats
  that and the "nearest free team-mate" baseline (0.269); 0.80 is out of reach for any model imitating this engine.
  The threshold stays as TECH_PLAN 1.6 sets it.
- **Clip review (20 strips):** no teleports (also 0 by count), team shapes hold (collapse 0.2% vs 0.35% scripted),
  keepers stay on their line and track the ball, no bunching into one cluster; ball progress is plausible (4 s FDE
  21.4 m vs 31.8 m velocity extrapolation, scripted engine 18.2 m on the earlier dry run).
- **Consequence:** `gate.json` = scripted_v1. Every cached counterfactual, Oracle forecast and Decision Index value
  uses scripted_v1; the policy ships as a documented experiment (TECH_PLAN 1.6 fallback, 1.13 row 1, M1 item 15).
